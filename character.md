# Lil Rappit — character bible

## Look (lock these in every prompt)
- Realistic, plush, light-grey furry rabbit; white fluffy chest; stocky, short body
- Tall ears with pink inner ear, left ear slightly torn/nicked at the tip
- Black bucket hat, always on
- Small gold-rimmed dark sunglasses
- Bright orange puffer jacket, open, no shirt underneath
- Thick gold Cuban-link chain with a small gold carrot pendant
- One gold front tooth
- Black baggy pants, white sneakers
- Props: chews a carrot like a cigar (no tobacco)

Reference images: `refs/turnaround.png` (front / 3-4 / side / back),
`refs/portrait.png` (face close-up), `refs/boss_chair.png` (boss pose).

## Personality
- Tough-guy gangster: slow, low voice, few words, intimidating stare
- One weakness: carrots. When he sees a bag of carrots he melts —
  ears droop, voice goes high and sweet, he agrees to a terrible deal.
- Catchphrase when melting: "...okay but it's the organic kind?"
- Recurring villain: Weasel — sly, smirking, knows his weakness

## Episode format (16 s = two 8 s clips)
1. **Tough (0–8 s):** Rappit in control, menacing, one hard line.
2. **Carrot (8–16 s):** a bag of carrots appears; he melts and accepts
   something ridiculous.

## Style
- Cinematic, photoreal, shallow depth of field, 9:16 vertical, 1080p
- Comedy timing: hold a beat of silence before the melt
- No rap. No smoking. Mild, family-safe crime parody only.
