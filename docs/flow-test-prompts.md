# Lil Rappit: Flow test prompts

## Setup (once)
1. In Flow, start a new project and pick **Omni Flash**, **9:16**, **10 s**.
   Use **360p** to test an idea (7 credits), then **720p** for a keeper (15 credits).
2. Add these images as **Ingredients** (reference images) and name them exactly:
   - **Rappit**: `rappit_turnaround.jpg` (add `rappit_portrait.jpg` too if you have room)
   - **Abu Lakma**: `abu_lakma.png`
   - **Lola**: `lola.png`
   - **Teta**: `teta.png`
3. Only add the ingredients a prompt uses (fewer references = more faithful characters).
4. Paste one prompt below. If it works, tell me in Discord with `idea …` or just post it.

Tip: every prompt names each prop before it is used, so nothing appears out of nowhere.
If something goes wrong, change only one thing per retry.

## 1. Zaytoun season (Rappit, Abu Lakma, Teta)
Ingredients: Rappit, Abu Lakma, Teta

> Cinematic photoreal 3D, vertical 9:16, warm golden afternoon light. A Lebanese olive grove on
> a terraced hillside, old stone walls, a big tarp spread under an olive tree, plastic crates.
> Rappit (gangster rabbit: black bucket hat, gold sunglasses, orange puffer, gold chain) and
> Abu Lakma (huge bulldog in a red velvet tracksuit) stand under the tree holding long sticks;
> Teta (tiny grandma rabbit, round glasses, cardigan, floral dress) sits on a plastic chair
> holding a cup of coffee.
> [0-4s] Rappit and Abu Lakma beat the branches with total mafia seriousness, olives raining
> down onto the tarp, slow-motion, dead-serious faces.
> [4-7s] Teta lifts one single olive from the tarp, inspects it, and shakes her head slowly
> in disappointment.
> [7-10s] Rappit and Abu Lakma freeze, look at each other, then both start beating the tree
> twice as fast, panicking; Abu Lakma glances into the camera.
> Sound: Lebanese folk music with oud and tabl, olives pattering on the tarp, Teta says
> "Ma fi zaytoun?" No other dialogue. No smoking, no weapons, no text on screen.

## 2. Power cut (Rappit, Abu Lakma)
Ingredients: Rappit, Abu Lakma

> Cinematic photoreal 3D, vertical 9:16. A Lebanese living room at night, a TV on, a ceiling
> lamp on. Rappit (gangster rabbit: black bucket hat, gold sunglasses, orange puffer, gold
> chain) stands in the middle of the room giving a dramatic serious speech to Abu Lakma (huge
> bulldog in a red velvet tracksuit) who sits on the sofa.
> [0-4s] Rappit points and speaks with intense mafia seriousness.
> [4-6s] The power cuts: total darkness, only Rappit's sunglasses glint. Outside, a generator
> coughs and starts.
> [6-10s] The lights flicker back on: Rappit is now standing on the coffee table in a
> completely different dramatic pose, still mid-speech, totally serious; Abu Lakma stares
> into the camera.
> Sound: tense orchestral music, the click of the power cut, the generator rumbling to life
> outside. Rappit says: "Ya3ni... where was I?" No smoking, no weapons, no text on screen.

## 3. Lola vs the carrot (drama: Rappit, Lola)
Ingredients: Rappit, Lola

> Cinematic photoreal 3D, vertical 9:16, soap-opera lighting. An elegant restaurant table by a
> window at night, candles, a plate with one perfect carrot on it in front of Rappit.
> Rappit (gangster rabbit: black bucket hat, gold sunglasses, orange puffer, gold chain) sits
> opposite Lola (glamorous white rabbit, long lashes, pink lipstick, pink faux-fur coat, gold
> hoop earrings, tiny handbag).
> [0-4s] Lola talks to Rappit lovingly, but he is staring only at the carrot on his plate.
> [4-7s] Lola notices, her face turns to dramatic shock; she slowly picks up the carrot from
> his plate and holds it next to her own face: "Ana walla hiye?" (me or her?)
> [7-10s] Rappit looks at Lola, then at the carrot, then at Lola again... and gently takes
> the carrot. Dramatic zoom on Lola's shocked face.
> Sound: soap-opera violin stings and a dramatic "dun dun dun" on the final zoom.
> No smoking, no weapons, no text on screen.

## 4. CCTV carrot heist (Rappit, Abu Lakma)
Ingredients: Rappit, Abu Lakma

> Grainy black-and-white night-vision security camera footage, high angle from a corner of
> the ceiling, timestamp-style framing (no readable text), vertical 9:16. A small Lebanese
> vegetable shop at night, crates of vegetables, a big crate of carrots in the middle.
> [0-4s] The door creaks open; Rappit (gangster rabbit: bucket hat, sunglasses, puffer jacket,
> chain) tiptoes in like a professional thief, crouching, while Abu Lakma (huge bulldog in a
> tracksuit) keeps watch at the door.
> [4-7s] Rappit reaches the carrot crate, lifts one carrot and holds it up like a diamond.
> [7-10s] Abu Lakma trips over a crate of potatoes; potatoes roll everywhere, the lights turn
> on, and both freeze, slowly looking straight up into the security camera.
> Sound: quiet ambient hum, creaking door, tiptoe sounds, a loud crash of potatoes.
> No dialogue, no weapons, no smoking, no readable text on screen.

## 5. The Lebanese goodbye (Rappit, Teta)
Ingredients: Rappit, Teta

> Cinematic photoreal 3D, vertical 9:16. The front door of Teta's house in a Lebanese village,
> warm interior light. Rappit (gangster rabbit: black bucket hat, gold sunglasses, orange
> puffer, gold chain) stands at the open door holding his car keys, ready to leave; Teta (tiny
> grandma rabbit, round glasses, cardigan, floral dress) holds his arm.
> [0-3s] Rappit says "Yalla, bye Teta" and steps toward the door; outside it is bright daylight.
> [3-7s] Teta keeps talking and handing him things from a side table: a bag of food, a jar of
> pickles, a tray of ma'amoul; he keeps nodding seriously, arms filling up; the light
> outside the door turns to sunset.
> [7-10s] It is now dark night outside; Rappit is still at the door, buried under food
> containers, sunglasses crooked, totally serious; Teta says "Ba3ed bakkir!" (it's still
> early!). He stares into the camera.
> Sound: gentle oud music, crickets at night. No smoking, no weapons, no text on screen.
