# The Interview
**Hook:** THE MOST FEARSOME CORPORATE NEGOTIATOR
**Deal:** He accepts an unpaid internship making coffee for everyone.
**Opponent:** A jittery, skinny red squirrel wearing a slightly oversized, wrinkled light-blue button-down shirt and a crooked red tie. He has big, anxious, darting brown eyes, twitchy whiskers, and messy ear tufts, looking perpetually sweaty and completely out of his depth.

## Shot 1 · THE DEMAND (4s)
- *Frame:* Close-up on Lil Rappit (a gangster rabbit in an orange puffer jacket, bucket hat, gold chain, and sunglasses), with the squirrel's blurred light-blue shoulder in the foreground right. A desk is partially visible.
- *Camera:* 85mm lens, slightly low angle, a quick and punchy push-in on Rappit's face.
- *Lighting:* Bright startup office background with out-of-focus neon signs, but a single hard motivated desk lamp casts deep dramatic shadows across half of Rappit's face, making his sunglasses glint sharply.
- *Action:* Rappit delivers his unyielding terms immediately from the very first frame, shutting down any room for debate.
- *Performance:* Rappit is deadpan, motionless, and radiating cold confidence. His jaw barely moves as he speaks, staring straight through the HR manager without a single blink.
- *Sound:* Low tense drone, ending with one heavy bass hit right on his last word.
> **Lil Rappit:** "Rappit don't do discounts. CEO and a corner office."

## Shot 2 · THE COUNTER (4s)
- *Frame:* Medium close-up on the squirrel HR manager over Rappit's blurred orange puffer shoulder in the foreground left. The desk is in focus between them.
- *Camera:* 85mm lens, eye-level, a slow, creeping push-in on the squirrel's terrified face.
- *Lighting:* Bright fluorescent startup lighting, flat and awkward, highlighting the squirrel's sweaty brow and the sterile office reality.
- *Action:* The squirrel wordlessly, shakily slides a massive silver platter across the desk. Sitting exactly in the center is one single tiny baby carrot.
- *Performance:* Extremely nervous and ashamed. The squirrel's shoulders are hunched. His eyes dart rapidly between Rappit's face and the desk, expecting this ridiculous move to fail. He does a prominent nervous swallow and offers a tiny, unsure glance up.
- *Sound:* The tense drone music drops out completely. Only the scrape of metal on wood, a loud nervous gulp, and heavy awkward silence.

## Shot 3 · THE MELT (6s)
- *Frame:* Extreme close-up on Rappit's stoic face, which dynamically pulls back to a wide two-shot showing both characters across the desk.
- *Camera:* Starts as an extreme close-up, then a steady pull-out back to a medium two-shot revealing the desk, the bean bags in the background, and both characters.
- *Lighting:* The hard shadow on Rappit's face miraculously lifts, replaced by a soft, glowing, even light that makes his eyes pop and sparkle.
- *Action:* Rappit gasps at second 2, clutching the tiny carrot with both paws. He eagerly slides a signed employment contract across the desk in the final two seconds. The squirrel slowly turns his head and stares directly into the camera in stunned disbelief.
- *Performance:* Dead serious for half a second. Then a HUGE over-the-top cartoon melt: sunglasses slide down his nose, eyes go enormous, sparkly and teary, ears shoot straight up then flop down, nose twitches fast, cheeks blush, lower lip trembles (peak melt between seconds 2 and 4). The squirrel looks stunned.
- *Sound:* A heavenly 'aaah' choir swell, a magical sparkle chime, and a playful pizzicato sting at the very end.
> **Lil Rappit:** "Carrots? Unpaid intern making everyone's coffee!"
