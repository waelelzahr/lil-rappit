# The Interview
**Hook:** THE TOUGHEST NEGOTIATOR IN CORPORATE
**Deal:** He trades his CEO demand for an unpaid internship making coffee just for one baby carrot.
**Opponent:** A jittery grey squirrel wearing a slightly oversized baby-blue button-down shirt and a crooked red tie, with wide nervous brown eyes and twitchy ears, looking exactly the same across all shots.

## Shot 1 · THE DEMAND (4s)
- *Frame:* Close-up of Lil Rappit wearing sunglasses, a bucket hat, orange puffer, and gold chain. The squirrel's shoulder is blurred in the foreground. The bright startup office has bean bags in the back.
- *Camera:* 85mm lens, slightly low angle, a quick punchy push-in directly on Lil Rappit's face.
- *Lighting:* One hard motivated window light creates a dramatic half-face shadow, giving a sharp glint on his sunglasses.
- *Action:* Rappit is already speaking the very instant the frame begins, delivering his demands with unbroken eye contact.
- *Performance:* Rappit projects total absolute authority. He does not hesitate or blink, his face completely rigid and devoid of emotion while he speaks.
- *Sound:* Low tense drone, finishing with one heavy bass hit exactly on his last word.
> **Lil Rappit:** "Rappit don't do discounts. CEO and a corner office."

## Shot 2 · THE COUNTER (4s)
- *Frame:* Medium close-up of the jittery squirrel HR manager across the desk, framed over Lil Rappit's blurred orange-puffer shoulder.
- *Camera:* 85mm lens, over-the-shoulder reverse shot, a slow creeping push-in on the squirrel.
- *Lighting:* Bright overhead office lighting that feels exposing, capturing every bead of nervous sweat on the squirrel.
- *Action:* The silent squirrel hesitantly slides a big silver plate bearing one single tiny baby carrot slowly across the desk toward Rappit.
- *Performance:* The squirrel looks deeply ashamed and embarrassed. Its eyes dart between Rappit's face and the floor. It swallows nervously, then offers a tiny, unsure glance up.
- *Sound:* The tense drone music drops out completely. The only sounds are the metallic scrape of the plate and a loud, nervous gulp in the awkward silence.

## Shot 3 · THE MELT (6s)
- *Frame:* Extreme close-up on Lil Rappit's stoic face, then pulling back to a wide two-shot showing both characters across the desk.
- *Camera:* Holds tight on Rappit for half a second, stays for the facial melt, then does a fast pull-back out to a wide two-shot over the final two seconds.
- *Lighting:* The harsh shadows immediately wash away, replaced by a soft, warm, glowing, angelic light on Rappit's face.
- *Action:* Rappit melts instantly upon seeing the bait. He squeaks his line, rapidly signs the employment contract with one paw, and pushes it back across the desk while clutching the tiny carrot to his chest.
- *Performance:* Dead serious for a split second, then a massive cartoon melt: sunglasses slide down, eyes go enormous and teary, ears shoot straight up then flop, nose twitches rapidly, cheeks blush, and lip trembles. The squirrel slowly turns to stare straight into the camera in stunned disbelief.
- *Sound:* A heavenly 'aaah' choir swell starts abruptly, followed by a sparkle chime, ending with a playful pizzicato sting.
> **Lil Rappit:** "Carrots? I'll be the unpaid coffee intern!"
