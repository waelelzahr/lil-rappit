# The Parking Spot
**Hook:** THE MAFIA FEARS THIS RABBIT
**Deal:** He gives up the only parking spot on the block AND washes the other driver's car.
**Opponent:** A tall, pushy flamingo with bright pink feathers, wearing a retro teal tracksuit jacket and a white sun visor, driving a comically tiny yellow convertible car. Large expressive beak and anxious, wide eyes.

## Shot 1 · THE DEMAND (4s)
- *Frame:* Close-up, slightly low angle on Lil Rappit in his orange puffer, bucket hat, gold chain, and sunglasses. The flamingo's teal-jacketed shoulder is blurred in the foreground. Background is a hazy, out-of-focus busy city street with cars.
- *Camera:* 85mm lens, slightly low angle, a quick punchy push-in.
- *Lighting:* One hard motivated streetlamp light, casting half of Rappit's face in deep shadow with a sharp glint on his sunglasses.
- *Action:* Rappit aggressively states his claim over the parking spot without a single moment of hesitation.
- *Performance:* Rappit is already talking in the first frame, deadpan and unblinking. His face is completely motionless except for his mouth, projecting absolute, quiet menace.
- *Sound:* Low tense drone, ending with one heavy bass hit on his last word.
> **Lil Rappit:** "Rappit don't do discounts. This spot is mine."

## Shot 2 · THE COUNTER (4s)
- *Frame:* Medium close-up on the flamingo sitting in the tiny yellow car, framed over Rappit's blurred orange shoulder. A slice of carrot cake on a fancy dessert plate rests on the car's door frame.
- *Camera:* A slow push-in over Rappit's blurred shoulder focusing on the flamingo's face.
- *Lighting:* Hard street light highlighting the flamingo's sweating face and the fancy dessert plate, with deep shadows in the car interior.
- *Action:* Hesitantly, the flamingo slides the slice of carrot cake on the fancy dessert plate across the car hood toward Rappit.
- *Performance:* The flamingo says absolutely nothing. They look deeply ashamed and embarrassed, eyes darting nervously between Rappit's face and the floor. A visible nervous swallow, followed by a tiny, unsure glance up.
- *Sound:* The music drops out completely. The only sounds are the ceramic plate scraping slowly across the metal car hood, a nervous gulp, and an awkward silence.

## Shot 3 · THE MELT (6s)
- *Frame:* Extreme close-up on Rappit's face, which pulls back at second 4 into a wide two-shot showing Rappit, the flamingo, the tiny car, and the empty parking spot.
- *Camera:* Starts static on the extreme close-up, then a fast, dramatic pull back to a two-shot.
- *Lighting:* The harsh shadows suddenly vanish, replaced by bright, even, colorful lighting.
- *Action:* Rappit melts at the sight of the cake. He grabs the plate with one paw, pulls out a soapy sponge with the other, and happily begins washing the tiny car to pay for the cake, surrendering the parking spot.
- *Performance:* 0-0.5s: Dead serious. 0.5s-4s: Huge cartoon melt; sunglasses slide down, eyes go enormous and sparkly, ears shoot straight up then flop, nose twitches fast, cheeks blush, lower lip trembles. 4s-6s: Rappit happily scrubs the car hood. The flamingo slowly turns to stare straight into the camera in stunned disbelief.
- *Sound:* A heavenly 'aaah' choir swell, a sparkle chime, and a playful pizzicato sting at the end.
> **Lil Rappit:** "Carrots? Okay, I will wash your car!"
