# Pink Slips
**Hook:** POV: THE DEALERSHIP OFFERS FREE MATS
**Deal:** He willingly hands over his own car keys before the street race even starts just for a bag of carrots.
**Opponent:** A sleek green racing lizard wearing a black leather jacket with red racing stripes. He stands upright, has a cocky, arrogant smirk, yellow reptilian eyes, and an overall slick, confident posture.

## Shot 1 · THE DEMAND (4s)
- *Frame:* Close-up of Lil Rappit in his orange puffer, bucket hat, gold chain, and sunglasses. The lizard's black leather shoulder is blurred in the foreground. Midnight street race setting, muscle car grilles visible in the background.
- *Camera:* 85mm lens, slightly low angle. A quick, punchy push-in directly onto Rappit's face.
- *Lighting:* One hard motivated light from a car headlight behind him; half his face is in deep shadow, sharp glint on his sunglasses, hazy night air.
- *Action:* Rappit smoothly and quickly issues his ultimate terms for the race without any hesitation.
- *Performance:* Rappit starts talking in the very first frame. Deadpan, no blinking, head tilted slightly down to look imposing and serious.
- *Sound:* Low tense drone, one heavy bass hit exactly on his last word.
> **Rappit:** "If I win, I take your car."

## Shot 2 · THE COUNTER (6s)
- *Frame:* Reverse shot over Rappit's blurred orange shoulder. The lizard stands confidently in front of a sleek muscle car hood.
- *Camera:* Sudden CRASH ZOOM onto a brown paper bag overflowing with fresh orange carrots that suddenly drops onto the car hood.
- *Lighting:* Hard headlight illumination cutting through the haze, casting long dramatic shadows. High contrast.
- *Action:* The lizard slides the bag of carrots onto the hood, waits a split second for the dramatic zoom, then delivers his counter-offer.
- *Performance:* The lizard drops the bag fast, flashes a sly, arrogant smile, knowing he has totally won.
- *Sound:* Dramatic 'dun dun DUN' sting perfectly timed with the crash zoom, loud paper bag crinkle, music cuts to dead silence right after his line.
> **Lizard:** "Take these, and give me your keys right now."

## Shot 3 · THE MELT (6s)
- *Frame:* Extreme close-up on Rappit's face, filling the frame. Headlight rim-light on his bucket hat.
- *Camera:* Static extreme close-up to capture every micro-expression of the facial transformation.
- *Lighting:* The harsh shadows suddenly soften, and a magical warm glowing light seems to reflect off his face from the carrots below.
- *Action:* Rappit instantly loses all his tough-guy composure, completely mesmerized and overwhelmed by the beautiful carrots.
- *Performance:* Dead serious for half a second. Then a huge over-the-top cartoon melt: sunglasses slide down his nose, eyes go enormous, sparkly and teary. Ears shoot straight up then flop. Nose twitches fast, cheeks blush bright red, lower lip trembles, paws clasp tightly under his chin. The face is at its most extreme and adorable from second 3.5 to 5.5.
- *Sound:* A heavenly 'aaah' choir swell and a magical sparkle chime.
> **Rappit:** "Okay mister, here you go!"

## Shot 4 · THE PAYOFF (4s)
- *Frame:* Wide two-shot showing both characters standing between the two idling muscle cars.
- *Camera:* Starts wide to show the exact exchange, then a fast push-in back to a close-up of Rappit's melted, blissed-out face to loop seamlessly.
- *Lighting:* Cinematic street race lighting, bright headlights cutting through thick midnight fog.
- *Action:* Rappit happily and willingly hands his own car keys over to the lizard before the race even starts, hugging his carrots in pure joy.
- *Performance:* The lizard looks extremely smug, pocketing the keys. Rappit is completely blissed out, eyes closed in ecstasy, hugging the heavy bag of carrots tight against his puffer jacket.
- *Sound:* A playful pizzicato string sting.
