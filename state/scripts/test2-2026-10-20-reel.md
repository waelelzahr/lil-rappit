# Pink Slips
**Hook:** POV: TRADING MY CAR AT THE DEALERSHIP
**Deal:** He willingly hands over the keys to his muscle car before the race even begins, entirely forfeiting, just to get a bag of carrots.
**Opponent:** A sleek, bright green iguana racing driver with a cocky, arrogant smirk. He wears a fitted black leather jacket with twin red racing stripes down the sleeves, a silver belt buckle, and has a swept-back scaly crest on his head.

## Shot 1 · THE DEMAND (4s)
- *Frame:* Close-up, slightly low angle on Lil Rappit in his orange puffer jacket, bucket hat, sunglasses, and gold chain. The iguana's leather-clad shoulder is blurred in the foreground. Background shows hazy midnight asphalt and muscle car grills.
- *Camera:* 85mm lens. A quick, punchy push-in directly onto Rappit's face.
- *Lighting:* Hard, intense motivated lighting from glaring car headlights slicing through thick street haze. Half of Rappit's face is in deep, stark shadow; his sunglasses sharply glint in the light.
- *Action:* Rappit speaks instantly on the very first frame without hesitation or blinking, asserting his dominance.
- *Performance:* Rappit is completely deadpan and intimidating, not moving a muscle except for his mouth. He exudes absolute, chilling confidence.
- *Sound:* Low, tense synthesizer drone that drops into one heavy, vibrating bass hit exactly on his final word.
> **Lil Rappit:** "When I win, I take your ride."

## Shot 2 · THE COUNTER (6s)
- *Frame:* Reverse over-the-shoulder shot favoring the green iguana. He stands in front of the illuminated hood of his muscle car.
- *Camera:* Starts as a medium shot, then a sudden, violent CRASH ZOOM right onto the paper bag of carrots as it hits the car hood.
- *Lighting:* Headlight beams cross behind the iguana, illuminating the haze and highlighting the bright, vibrant orange of the fresh carrots.
- *Action:* At second 1, the iguana slams a brown paper bag overflowing with bright orange carrots onto the car hood. The camera crash-zooms on it. He smiles slyly and states his counter-offer.
- *Performance:* The iguana smirks confidently. He slaps the bag down with a dramatic, practiced flair, locking eyes with Rappit.
- *Sound:* A dramatic, soap-opera style 'dun dun DUN' sting triggers on the crash zoom. Loud paper crinkle. Music cuts to total silence after his line.
> **Iguana:** "Skip the race. Give me your keys for these carrots."

## Shot 3 · THE MELT (6s)
- *Frame:* Extreme close-up on Lil Rappit's face, filling the entire frame.
- *Camera:* Static extreme close-up to perfectly capture the facial transformation without distraction.
- *Lighting:* The harsh headlight shadowing vanishes, replaced by a soft, glowing, magical, even light.
- *Action:* Rappit completely loses his gangster persona. He melts into a puddle of absolute joy at the sight of the carrots, entirely ignoring the terrible terms of the deal.
- *Performance:* Dead serious for 0.5s. Then, a massive, exaggerated melt: sunglasses slide down his nose, his eyes become enormous, sparkly, and teary. His ears shoot straight up then instantly flop down. His nose twitches frantically, cheeks blush bright pink, his lower lip trembles. His paws clasp together under his chin. The expression hits its absolute maximum adorable extreme between seconds 3.5 and 5.5.
- *Sound:* A heavenly 'aaah' choir swell perfectly synced with a magical sparkle chime.
> **Lil Rappit:** "Ooh! Yes, take my keys!"

## Shot 4 · THE PAYOFF (4s)
- *Frame:* Wide two-shot of the cars' hoods. Rappit is happily hugging the bag of carrots to his chest. The iguana stands opposite him.
- *Camera:* Starts wide, then smoothly pushes in to end on a tight close-up of Rappit's blissed-out, melted face, setting up a perfect visual loop back to shot 1.
- *Lighting:* Back to the realistic street racing lighting: harsh headlights, strong shadows, and midnight haze.
- *Action:* Rappit happily tosses his own car keys across the hood to the iguana while tightly clutching the bag of carrots. The iguana snatches the keys, and the camera pushes in on Rappit's completely oblivious, happy face.
- *Performance:* Rappit is completely lost in carrot euphoria, eyes closed in pure bliss. The iguana looks incredibly smug, shaking his head in disbelief at his easy victory.
- *Sound:* A light, playful, comedic pizzicato string sting.
